# Visual assets

PinPoint uses an original wordmark and a simple star symbol. It does not include an official Pinterest or Google logo.

The bundled demonstration photographs were downloaded from Unsplash image URLs. File-to-source mappings are recorded in data/image-sources.json. They illustrate a small synthetic catalog; the app does not scrape Pinterest, fetch Pinterest content, or offer a stock-photo download service.
